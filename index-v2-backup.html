I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. Checked October 17, 2026

What I found

01

Your contact page is slow to appear on a phone.

In a simulated slow-connection load test on a phone-size screen, the contact page scored 54 and 62 out of 100. Its first content showed at 3.6 and 3.3 seconds and its largest item finished painting at 6.8 and 6.7 seconds; that item was the cookie notice text. The page transferred 1,362,747 bytes (about 1.4 MB) in 48 requests, including four Google Maps script files of roughly 72 to 96 KB each. Your booking page scored 91 and 89, and your hormone therapy page 91 in one run. One test setup, two runs on the contact and booking pages; field speed is unmeasured.

02

A 433 KB payment-plan script loads on every page I tested, with no compression and no caching.

The Cherry payment widget (files.withcherry.com/widgets/widget.js) is 432,939 bytes and is sent uncompressed; Lighthouse estimates 308 KiB could be saved. Its cache lifetime is 0, so a returning visitor downloads it again. It loaded on all four pages I tested: the homepage, booking, contact and hormone therapy pages. On the homepage it is 43% of the 1,006,671 bytes the page transferred. I didn't open the Cherry calculator, so I can't say what a visitor sees when it runs.

03

Your homepage takes about 3.3 seconds to show its main image on a phone, and most of that is waiting.

In the same test the homepage scored 87 and 78 out of 100. Its largest item, the hero picture, finished at 3.3 seconds in both runs. Of that, 2.7 seconds (81%) was render delay rather than download time, because the server answered in 0.6 seconds. Total blocking time was 112 and 404 milliseconds, and the page became fully interactive at 7.2 and 8.7 seconds. Two runs, one test setup.

04

Some faded text on your homepage is too light to read comfortably.

The automatic accessibility check scored the homepage 96 out of 100 and flagged 27 elements for low contrast. One example is large heading text, color #909fa2 on #f4f2ed, with a contrast ratio of 2.44 where 3 is the minimum for text that size; its opacity is set to 45%. The same check flagged the floating payment-calculator button, whose visible text isn't part of its accessible name “Open payment calculator”. This is an automatic check, not a person using a screen reader.

05

Three “Learn more” links on your homepage all go to the same page.

Lighthouse's link-text check flagged three links on the homepage whose text is only “Learn more”, and all three point to /services/concierge-longevity. This is the only failing item in the homepage SEO score of 92, while your booking, contact and hormone therapy pages scored 100. Links that say where they go, such as the program name, are easier for visitors and for search tools to understand.

06

Your site wasn't named in four of the five searches I tried, though it was named for your own name.

I searched the way a patient might: stem cell therapy in Las Vegas, a longevity clinic in Henderson, testosterone therapy in Henderson, and NAD+ IV therapy in Las Vegas. Each answer named four or five other clinics or services and none named you. A search for GIOSTAR Longevity & Wellness Henderson NV did name you, with your address, phone and services correct. Your clinic opened on September 30, 2026, so this is an early snapshot. The full table is in the AI search section below.

    Business markup matches the page

  • Found
